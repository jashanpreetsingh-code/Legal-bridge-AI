WHEREAS the First Party is the lawful owner and in absolute possession of the Second Floor residential unit situated at House No. 412, Sector 15-A, Chandigarh, comprising Two Bedrooms with attached bathrooms, Living/Dining Hall, and Modular Kitchen (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHEDULED PREMISES").

AND WHEREAS the Second Party has approached the First Party to take the Scheduled Premises on rent for bona fide residential purposes only, and the First Party has agreed to let out the same on the following terms and mutual covenants:

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. TENANCY PERIOD & TERM:
The tenancy shall be for a fixed duration of 11 (Eleven) calendar months, commencing on 1st November 2026 and expiring on 30th September 2027. Any extension shall be subject to mutual written consent and fresh execution of an agreement.

2. MONTHLY RENT & PAYMENT TERMS:
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of ₹18,500/- (Eighteen Thousand Five Hundred Rupees only) for the Scheduled Premises. The rent shall be payable strictly in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month via NEFT / UPI / Bank Transfer into the Landlord's designated bank account.

3. LATE PAYMENT PENALTY:
If the monthly rent is not paid by the 7th day of the current month, a default penalty of ₹250/- (Two Hundred Fifty Rupees) per day shall be levied from the due date until full clearance. Delay exceeding 20 days shall entitle the Landlord to terminate the agreement forthwith.

4. INTEREST-FREE SECURITY DEPOSIT:
Prior to taking physical possession, the Tenant has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of ₹37,000/- (Thirty-Seven Thousand Rupees only, equivalent to two months' rent) via bank transaction. The said deposit shall be refunded upon peaceful handover of vacant possession, subject to deduction of:
(a) Unpaid utility bills (electricity, water, Wi-Fi);
(b) Documented actual cost of repairing structural or fixture damage beyond ordinary wear and tear;
(c) Professional painting/deep cleaning charges if premises are returned in soiled condition.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD & NOTICE FOR TERMINATION:
(a) Both parties agree to an initial lock-in period of 3 (three) months during which neither party may terminate the tenancy.
(b) After the lock-in period, either party may terminate the tenancy by providing 30 (thirty) days' prior notice in writing or rent in lieu thereof.
(c) If the Tenant vacates the premises without serving the stipulated 30-day notice, one month's rent shall be deducted from the security deposit.

6. UTILITY CHARGES & OUTGOINGS:
The Tenant shall be liable to pay the following recurring charges in addition to the monthly rent:
(a) Electricity consumption charges as per actual sub-meter reading at prevailing PSPCL/Electricity Department rates.
(b) Fixed municipal water and sewerage maintenance charges of ₹500/- per month.
(c) Personal broadband internet, DTH, and LPG cylinder expenses.

7. REPAIRS & MAINTENANCE:
(a) Day-to-day minor repairs costing up to ₹1,500/- per occurrence (including bulb replacements, tap washer leaks, minor fuse repairs) shall be borne exclusively by the Tenant.
(b) Major structural maintenance (seepage, main electrical panel failure, building exterior repairs) shall be attended to by the Landlord upon written notice.

8. PROHIBITED USES & CODE OF CONDUCT:
(a) The premises shall be used exclusively for private residential dwelling. Commercial, industrial, or illegal activities are strictly forbidden.
(b) The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, mortgage, or part with possession of any portion of the premises to any third party.
(c) Nuisance, offensive trades, or storage of hazardous, inflammable, or explosive materials is strictly prohibited.

9. LANDLORD'S RIGHT OF ENTRY & INSPECTION:
The Landlord or his authorized representative reserves the right to inspect the condition of the premises by providing 24 (twenty-four) hours' prior notice (oral or via messaging), except in situations of fire, pipe bursts, or emergency where immediate entry is necessary.

10. JURISDICTION & DISPUTE RESOLUTION:
In the event of any dispute or difference arising out of or in connection with this agreement, the parties shall endeavor to resolve it amicably. Unresolved disputes shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and the courts at Chandigarh / Mohali shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
